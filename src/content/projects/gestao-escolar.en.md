## Status

Project closed in February 2026. The idea started in my undergraduate thesis (TCC), was
explored with two classmates for about two years, and didn't move forward as a maintained
product — it stays here as an architecture and product-decision case study.

## Why I built this

It was the kind of problem that interested me: data that already exists, poorly used, with
a direct impact on the people who depend on it. It was also a chance to exercise product
and architecture decisions end to end, not just the interface layer — even without the
project becoming a market product.
